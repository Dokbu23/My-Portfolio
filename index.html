Discover Mansalay

UI/UX design and front-end build for a mobile-first tourism platform

Discover Mansalay promotes ecotourism, cultural heritage, local enterprises, and accommodations in Mansalay, Oriental Mindoro. I designed the experience so visitors can find places on a map, plan trips, and ask an AI assistant for help, while resort owners and local businesses can manage their own pages without technical skills.

Designed for four types of users

Tourists

Browse attractions, events, resorts, and local products. Save a wishlist and build a day-by-day itinerary.

Resort owners

Manage lodging profiles, rooms, rates, photos, and traveler inquiries.

Local enterprises

Show handmade crafts and local produce with a public business profile.

Administrators

Review and publish content, verify merchants, and track platform activity.

  • Mobile-first layouts that work on phones, tablets, and desktop
  • Interactive map for finding resorts, landmarks, and cultural sites
  • Separate dashboards and flows for tourists, owners, businesses, and admins
  • Built in React, TypeScript, and Tailwind CSS, with a Laravel and MySQL backend
